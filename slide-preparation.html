Pharmaceutical Lab · Emulsion Preparation
METHOD OF PREPARATION
01
🥴
Oil Phase
Porcelain Dish · Water Bath
  • Melt Beeswax in a porcelain dish on a water bath at 🌡️ 75°C
  • Add Mineral Oil and stir gently to combine into a uniform oily phase
02
⚗️
Aqueous Phase
Glass Beaker · Heat Treatment
  • Dissolve Borax completely in distilled water inside a glass beaker
  • Heat the solution to 🌡️ 75°C to ensure full dissolution and stability
03
🔬
Emulsification
Continuous Mixing · Final Product
  • Add borax solution to the oily phase with constant stirring
  • Mix on water bath for 4 – 5 minutes, then remove from heat
  • Continue mixing until a smooth cream is formed ✨
